The story I'll always remember

“Gaadi bahar nikalo.”

Sir, first class mein aapne ek story sunayi thi. Us waqt shayad woh sirf ek simple story lagi thi... lekin jaise-jaise waqt guzra, uska meaning aur zyada samajh aaya.

Ek garage mein saari gaadiyan khadi thi aur wait kar rahi thi...

Sab soch rahi thi ki jab saare signals green ho jayenge, tab garage se bahar niklenge.

Lekin problem ye thi...

Jab tak koi gaadi garage se bahar nahi niklegi, tab tak pata kaise chalega ki saare signals green ho gaye?

Isliye...

What that story taught me

Shayad isi liye
woh story yaad reh gayi.

Life mein hum aksar wait karte rehte hain — perfect opportunity ka, perfect time ka, perfect confidence ka, perfect conditions ka.


Lekin aapki us chhoti si story ne ek simple cheez samjha di:


Agar hum hamesha green signal ka wait karte rahenge, toh shayad kabhi garage se bahar hi nahi nikalenge.


Isliye try karo. Start karo. Create karo. Fail ho toh dobara karo.

Bas... gaadi bahar nikalo.
